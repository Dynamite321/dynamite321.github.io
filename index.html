2026

Geometry-Based RLVR Credit Assignment from Hidden-State Trajectories

Yanjun Fu, Shunchi Zhang, Zhongtao Jiang, Kun Xu, Sanghamitra Dutta

Preprint, 2026

Preprint coming soon.

TL;DR

Uses hidden-state geometry for step-level credit assignment in RLVR. On AIME 2024–2026, our method outperforms DAPO by an average of 2.38 points and surpasses DAPO's peak performance with 1.31× fewer GPU hours, placing it on the performance–compute Pareto frontier among the evaluated RLVR methods.

Keywords: RLVR · Credit assignment · Interpretability

LogT: Logically Think with Images for Visual Search

Yanjun Fu, Quanzeng You, Jiadong Guo, Yujie Lu, Sai Charitha Akula, Haiping Wu, Miao Liu, Sanghamitra Dutta, Lu Yuan

Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS), 2026

Paper Code coming soon.

TL;DR

Proposes an agentic data engine that synthesizes SFT data for VLM tool use. Using SFT alone, LogT-SFT surpasses the SOTA model trained with both SFT and RL by 1.9 points, and LogT-RL surpasses the SOTA by 9.6 points.

Keywords: Agentic data synthesis for VLM agents

2025

T-Shirt: Token-Selective Hierarchical Data Selection for Instruction Tuning

Yanjun Fu, Faisal Hamman, Sanghamitra Dutta

Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS), 2025

Paper Code

TL;DR

Selects SFT examples by focusing on informative tokens and checking quality consistency among similar samples. Models trained on just 5% of the data can outperform those trained on the full dataset. The method is also highly scalable: it processes 52k samples in just 40 minutes on a single GPU.

Keywords: Data selection for SFT

Few-Shot Knowledge Distillation of LLMs With Counterfactual Explanations

Faisal Hamman, Pasan Dissanayake, Yanjun Fu, Sanghamitra Dutta

Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS), 2025

Paper Code

TL;DR

Uses minimally changed examples that flip a teacher model's prediction to help a smaller model learn its decision boundary. This improves knowledge distillation with very little training data.

Keywords: Knowledge distillation · Counterfactual explanations
